Field safety & legal cautions

Seasons are NOT shown as clearance

Germany’s Bundesjagdgesetz § 22 protects animals outside permitted seasons and includes protection for adults needed to rear dependent young. Hessen revised hunting regulations from 1 April 2026. Before hunting, confirm the actual species, state, Revier, applicable orders, disease controls and any Abschussplan with authoritative local sources.

Wild meat and abnormalities

Do not use suspect wild meat as food until officially assessed. The current Tier-LMHV § 2b requires Trichinella testing for wild boar and other susceptible animals in the circumstances covered by the law. Observe current veterinary and food-inspection procedures; a card cannot replace a kundige Person assessment or the competent authority.

African swine fever (ASP)

Restrictions and reporting requirements can change by district. The Friedrich-Loeffler-Institut publishes maps, but warns that its own maps are not necessarily updated daily. Check the competent veterinary authority and current local ASP rules. Do not move or handle suspicious carcasses contrary to official instructions.

Source hierarchy

Animal descriptions: current DJV species profiles where individually checked; otherwise explicitly named GHC course slide baseline. Legal matters: current federal and Hessian official publications, not historical course handouts. Geographic and temporal details must be rechecked when online.
