# Course-slide wording vs. current independent references — 2026-10-13

This is a **separate web-source correction record**, not part of the slide-only Details or Other Facts curriculum. It identifies cases where faithfully repeating old or simplified slide wording could mislead a hunter. Always verify the rules in the hunting location, with the competent authority and appropriate authorization.

## Wolf: course's "no season" line must not be interpreted as current law

- Primary course slide checked: **Game - Predators (202609).pdf**, Wolf slide. It says Wolf is now legal game but has "no season".
- **Current independent federal reference:** [Federal Hunting Act (BJagdG), §22](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bjagdg/__22.html) includes conditional hunting of wolves **1 July–31 October** when the statutory management-plan conditions are met. The Act's wolf-specific protections and restrictions are in [§22b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bjagdg/__22b.html) and related provisions. This is **not** a general open season or permission to take a wolf.
- [Federal Environment Ministry, FAQ last updated 10 April 2026](https://www.bundesumweltministerium.de/faq/koennen-woelfe-bejagt-werden): the amendment entered into force **2 April 2026**, adding the wolf to hunting law while it remains a protected species under the FFH framework.
- App correction: wolf card now explicitly attributes the "no season" wording to the **Fall 2026 slide**, instead of presenting it as a current blanket legal conclusion. Consult state rules and any specific plan/permit before an actual hunt.

## Raccoon: "small bear" is not a taxonomic classification

- Primary slide checked: **Game - Predators (202609).pdf**, Waschbär slide. It says "Is a small bear, not a dog!"
- Independent taxonomy reference: [NCBI Taxonomy — Procyonidae](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=9647&mode=Info) and [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service taxonomic tree](https://www.fws.gov/taxonomic-tree/32129) list raccoon in **Procyonidae**, not the bear family **Ursidae**.
- App correction: identifies "small bear" as the class's shorthand only, not a literal family assignment. Do not memorize it as formal taxonomy.

## Bird-identification correction

- Primary **Game - Federwild.pdf** (2020) duck-type passage describes **Spiegel** as an iridescent **wing** patch of dabbling ducks.
- The app's former wording incorrectly labeled it a *rump* patch, which is an identification problem. This was corrected in the Stockente study card.
- Hunting seasons and huntability shown in this **2020** deck are historical class content. Do not assume state rules remain the same in October 2026.
