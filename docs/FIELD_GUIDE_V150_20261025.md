The German Hunting Course has been completed. The Android app now launches **Jäger Field Guide** (an offline-first, field-oriented animal reference), while keeping the original study/quiz app at **Study archive**. Do not delete, rewrite or remap existing practice banks or their progress identifiers.

- Package ID stays \`org.ghcstudy.v141\`; v1.5.0 uses \`versionCode 151\`.

## Non-negotiable interpretation
**A species listed under German hunting law is not automatically a currently huntable species.** Even a legal open season does not necessarily permit taking an individual animal; dependence of young, protected status, state/local orders, land-specific permissions, disease zones and management plans matter. Do not put current/open/closed hunting status on a field card without current authoritative geographic validation.

**Field identification is not positive carcass diagnosis.** Unusual behavior, symptoms, lesions and contamination demand appropriate veterinary/official follow-up. Do not use wild boar meat before required Trichinella clearance.

**No false photograph attribution.** The current field guide uses already packaged course and mount imagery and visibly states that these are not field photos; missing photo slots stay labelled. Don't scrape/copy web photos into this public GitHub repository without permission and correct attribution.
