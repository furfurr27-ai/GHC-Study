# GHC Study glossary: nine source-linked definitions (2026-10-13)

## Audit scope
The nine German headwords below appear in the GHC `Jägersprache1.xlsx` worksheet's term index. Their English meanings are supported to the degree indicated by directly inspected GHC course-PDF text. This is a **meaning/usage check**, not historical etymology research or a present-day law check. Leave the `etymology` field blank when origin is unproven.

| Headword (worksheet spelling where applicable) | Meaning | Directly checked course evidence | Evidence quality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brucellose | Brucellosis | `Disease - Bacteria Viruses.pdf` identifies Brucellosis/Brucellose and `Brucella abortus/melitensis/suis`; marks it bacterial and zoonotic | Direct class-text terminology |
| Schweinepest | Classical swine fever | Same disease deck lists Classical Swine Fever (Schweinepest), distinct from African Swine Fever | Direct class-text terminology |
| Fuchsbandwurm | Fox tapeworm | `Game - Fox.pdf` lists Fuchsbandwurm as associated disease; `Disease - Parasites.pdf` identifies fox tapeworm and alveolar echinococcosis | Course terminology and matching parasitology |
| Kleiner Muensterlaender | Small Münsterländer (pointing dog) | `Hunting Dogs (202609).pdf`, `Know the Dogs and their Uses`, lists `Kleiner or Großer Münsterländer` among `Vorstehhunde (Pointers)` | Direct class-text breed category |
| Hahn in Ruh | Guns-at-rest/cease-fire signal | `Hunting Techniques (20260216).pdf` lists `Hahn in Ruh` among drive-hunt start and stop signals at the `Jagdleiter` briefing | Contextual class-text identification as a stop signal |
| Untere Jagdbehoerde | Lower/local hunting authority | `Range - Revier (20260922).pdf` identifies `Subordinate Hunting Authority (Unterejagdbehörde - UJB)` | Direct class-text institution; worksheet spelling differs |
| Jagdschein | Hunting license | `Hunting Techniques (20260216).pdf` drive-hunt document checklist requires `Jagdschein`; `Law - Hunting (202608).pdf` also names Jagdscheins | **Contextual**: German word is on slides, explicit English gloss is not in inspected passage |
| Wiederkäuer | Ruminant | `Game - Hares & Rabbits (202204).pdf` compares hares' single stomach with four-chambered ruminants, worksheet contains `Wiederkäuer` | **Contextual**: English biological meaning on slide, German headword on worksheet |
| Frettieren | Ferreting (rabbit hunting with ferrets) | `Game - Hares & Rabbits (202204).pdf` lists ferrets as rabbit-hunting aids, worksheet contains the verb `Frettieren` | **Contextual**: method on slide, German verb on worksheet |
