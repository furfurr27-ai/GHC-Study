**Jägersprache context and possible English renderings:**

- **Bache (female wild boar):** `Haken` are her canine teeth, a female-boar tooth/trophy term. Both [Waidwissen](https://waidwissen.com/lexikon/h) and [Jagdsignale](https://jagdsignale.com/jagdlexikon/h/) directly corroborate this sense; the associated boar vocabulary is [Waidwissen Schwarzwild](https://waidwissen.com/lexikon/schwarzwild).
- **Hirscharten (deer):** some hunting dictionaries also use `Haken` for vestigial canine teeth (`Grandeln`). Do **not** assume a specific displayed tooth is from deer without checking the source animal.
- **Hase (hare):** some dictionaries use `Haken` for a fleeing hare's sudden sharp change of direction. This is behavior, **not** a trophy tooth.
