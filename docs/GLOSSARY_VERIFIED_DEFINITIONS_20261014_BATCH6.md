# GHC Study — four independently sourced worksheet definitions (2026-10-14)

The term spellings are from the existing connected `Jägersprache1.xlsx` index. These translations are **not claimed to be taught under those exact German headwords in the 2026 class slides** unless explicitly noted. All four records keep `etymology` empty: none of the sources proves a historical origin or a poacher's code word.

| Indexed term | English meaning | Exact cross-check and source category |
|---|---|---|
| `x103 Epagneul Breton` | Brittany Spaniel (also Brittany) | **FCI breed registry #95** identifies `EPAGNEUL BRETON` as `BRITTANY SPANIEL` (English), `BRETONISCHER SPANIEL` (German), pointing-dog Group 7: https://www.fci.be/en/nomenclature/BRITTANY-SPANIEL-95.html . The connected `Hunting Dogs (202609).pdf` also lists Brittany among pointing breeds without using this exact French headword. **official_breed_external_plus_class_context** |
| `x087 Blindschleiche` | Slow worm, a legless lizard (*Anguis fragilis*) | **Bundesamt für Naturschutz** identifies `Blindschleiche` as a legless lizard, not a snake: https://naturdetektive.bfn.de/fileadmin/NATDET/documents/Kapitel_1-10/15884-15934-1-kapitel3_lebensr_ume.pdf . **Natural History Museum (London)** gives the English `slow worm` and matching Latin species: https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/slow-worms-most-unusual-lizards-in-britain.html . **official_nature_external** |
| `x124 Kreuzotter` | Common European adder / common viper (*Vipera berus*) | **EPPO Global Database** explicitly pairs German `Kreuzotter` with English `adder`, genus/species `Vipera berus`: https://gd.eppo.int/taxon/VIPEBE . The connected older `Law - Nature and Animal (201902).pdf` uses the English `European Adder` in the protected snake section without supplying this German worksheet headword. **official_taxonomy_external_plus_class_context** |
| `x089 Borkenkaefer` | Bark beetle (group of beetles developing beneath bark) | `Waldwissen.net`, published by German and other forestry research agencies, directly pairs `Borkenkäfer` with `bark beetles`, and explains their tree impacts: https://www.waldwissen.net/en/forestry/forest-protection/insects/bark-beetles-faq . **forest_research_external** |

No current hunting authorization, legal quota or disease diagnosis is inferred from these definitions. The FCI nomenclature is a **breed identification**, not a statement that the breed is allowed for any specific hunt or certificate. `Blindschleiche` is **not a snake**, despite its snake-like shape.
