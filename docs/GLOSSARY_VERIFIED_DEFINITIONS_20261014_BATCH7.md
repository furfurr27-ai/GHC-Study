# GHC Study — five source-attributed Jägersprache terms (2026-10-14)

The five German headwords originate in the existing classroom `Jägersprache1.xlsx` worksheet. Their English descriptions were verified using distinct sources **outside the GHC lecture slides**. No etymology is asserted. Their `definitionAudit` flags distinguish government examination wording, lexicography, veterinary authority and other hunting education from text directly translated out of the class slides.

| German worksheet headword | English / functional meaning | Source directly checked | Evidence limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Wechselwild` (x156) | Transient game that does not live continuously in one Revier; may move between different hunting grounds | [Hessen RP Kassel official Jägerprüfungsfragen Sachgebiet 2, 2023-11-23, question 106](https://rp-kassel.hessen.de/sites/rp-kassel.hessen.de/files/2023-11/2023-11-23-jaegerpruefungsfragen_sachgebiet_2_jagdbetrieb.pdf) | Official exam terminology; not equivalent to a physical path, and not a proven GHC slide translation |
| `Wildwechsel` (x160) | Regularly used game path or crossing | [Duden Wildwechsel](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Wildwechsel), with Hessen official exam question 74 describing game crossing context | A movement path, NOT the animals temporarily entering a hunting ground; links to x156 |
| `Moderhinke` (x130) | Footrot; infectious, painful disease of hooves/claws, which can affect mouflon | [AGES veterinary disease factsheet](https://ages.at/mensch/krankheit/krankheitserreger-von-a-bis-z/moderhinke), [Hessen 2022 official Jägerprüfungsfragen Sachgebiet 1, question 130](https://rp-kassel.hessen.de/sites/rp-kassel.hessen.de/files/2022-05/2022-05-12-jp-fragenkatalog-sachgebiet_1.pdf), [Deutscher Jagdverband mouflon species page](https://www.jagdverband.de/zahlen-fakten/tiersteckbriefe/mufflon-ovis-ammon-musimon) | Species/classification and disease vocabulary only, NOT a diagnosis from a picture, clinical treatment protocol or current disease-reporting law |
| `Wolfssprosse` (x161) | An occasional extra tine of the red-deer antler between the middle tine and crown | [Swiss BEJV Wildkunde Rotwild course PDF, page 20](https://jagdausbildung-bejv.ch/download/5990/05-wildkunde-ml/1AeLewrqwc3tC6LBp7vQ3yaX_-WsaKXZX/Wildkunde%205%2C%202021%2C%20LZ%205.2.1.1-1%2C%20Rotwild.pdf), [Waidwissen glossary](https://waidwissen.com/lexikon/wolfssprosse) | Verified external hunters' anatomical term; do not confuse with Wolfskralle (dog dewclaw). No historical etymology asserted |
| `Fuehrende` (x111) | A female roe/red deer accompanied by or caring for dependent offspring (e.g. a leading Ricke/Alttier) | [Hessen RP Kassel hunter-exam questions Sachgebiet 2, 2023-01-10, questions 132 and 133](https://rp-kassel.hessen.de/sites/rp-kassel.hessen.de/files/2023-01/jaegerpruefungsfragen_sachgebiet_2.pdf) | Contextual *führende Ricke / führendes Alttier* rather than an exact unqualified worksheet translation or a general interpretation of dog handling, herd leadership or a legal shooting permission |
