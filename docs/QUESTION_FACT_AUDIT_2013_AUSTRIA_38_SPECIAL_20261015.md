# GHC Study — source comparisons: 2013 Austria hunting travel and .38 Special ammunition
Reviewed: 15 October 2026

## Directly inspected primary sources

1. **Original examination** — connected Google Drive [`Practice Hunting Test  2013-12-19.pdf`](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1f_bWpni_W7xpReNHPHCWittNiQSBFOwX/view), printed `PART III / Weapons and Firearms Law` questions **6** and **20** and printed original **Section 3** answer table. Q6 keys **A/YES** for .38 Special in a .357 Magnum revolver; Q20 keys **B** for Jagdschein + WBK + European Firearms Pass + passport + Austrian Jagdschein. Verified readable original PDF text; source page pixels were not rendered in this turn.
2. **Manufacturer primary source** — [Ruger Security-Six, Speed-Six, Police Service-Six Instruction Manual](https://ruger-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/_manuals/securitySix.pdf), manufacturer hosted, opened and **actual PDF screenshot on printed page 4 / zero-index page 3 visually inspected**. Its `AMMUNITION` passage specifies a .357 Magnum-marked revolver can also chamber .38 Special; a revolver marked .38 Special should use only .38 Special, and a 9mm-marked chamber has its own separate ammunition rules. This verifies the **direction** of compatibility but not all possible firearm models or reloaded ammunition.
3. **Austrian government official hunting credentials** — [`Jagdkarte` on oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/lexicon/J/Seite.991741), last updated **16 January 2026**, directly opened/read. Actual Austrian authority to hunt requires relevant Jagdkarte (annual) or Jagdgastkarte (guest card), landowner/licenseholder hunt permission and conditions of the individual **Bundesland**; issuance varies by province.
4. **Austrian Interior Ministry firearm-travel guidance** — [bringing weapons into Austria from another EU member state](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/en/themen/gesetze_und_recht/waffenrecht/1/Seite.2450210), official update **28 April 2026**, directly opened/read. For EU residents temporarily bringing registered firearms, European Firearms Pass (EFP) and potentially travel authorization; specific **category-C hunting** exceptions when entered in EFP and with documented hunting purpose. Crucially **EFP does not authorize purchasing/carrying** firearms or substitute for the *Austrian hunting entitlement*.
5. **Austrian Finance Ministry customs** — [carrying weapons](https://www.bmf.gv.at/en/topics/customs/travellers/prohibitions-and-restrictions-on-imports/carrying-weapons.html), official customs page, directly read; identifies hunter simplifications for qualifying weapons and travel purposes, not general exemptions for handguns.
6. **Provincial statutory examples** — Austrian official RIS publications [`NÖ Jagdgesetz` §59](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=LrNO&Anlage=&Artikel=&Gesetzesnummer=20000559&Paragraf=59) (guest card qualification and guest-license insurance) and [`Oö Jagdgesetz 2024` §30](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=LrOO&Anlage=&Artikel=&Gesetzesnummer=20001285&Paragraf=30) (must have valid provincial Jagdkarte or Jagdgastkarte). Their text was available in search results, **but the NÖ direct RIS page returned HTTP 403**, so do **not** claim full current NÖ statute directly read; provincial legislation is time-sensitive. An official Austrian government page already independently confirms province-specific differences.
