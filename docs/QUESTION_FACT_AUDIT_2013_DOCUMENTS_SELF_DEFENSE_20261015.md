## Item-level audit

| Stable question ID | Original paper key | Source-qualified status |
|---|---|---|
| `P2013-S3-Q05` borrowed hunting rifle during driven hunt | **A,C,D** | **Qualified**: original license + identity + written temporary-loan proof aligns with WaffG §38(1)(1)(f),(2), §12(1)(1) and §13(4) in relevant circumstances. §38 identifies parties/date; original paper includes loaned weapon's details, a sensible documentary addition, not claimed to be a universal text-for-text statutory requirement. No general WBK for the borrowed long gun when statutory exemption applies; borrowing still has prerequisites. |
| `P2013-S3-Q09` acquire .308 hunting rifle at dealer | **B** | **Dated/overbroad key**: paper says existing WBK + Jagdschein + ID. Current WaffG §13(3) explicitly allows *qualifying lawful hunting long guns* to be acquired without a prior acquisition permit by eligible **annual** Jagdschein holders, followed by application for WBK issuance/entry within **two weeks**. Historic B preserved and **warning before answering**. Annual license scope, WBK follow-up, retailer/foreign military procedures still apply; do not silently make paper's C a universal alternate answer. |
| `P2013-S3-Q10` buy 9mm Luger handgun from private person | **C** | **Qualified**: an individual handgun requires appropriate prior acquisition authorization entered in a WBK under §10(1). WaffG §13(2) may recognize hunters' legitimate needs but the special no-prior-permit long-gun purchase exception §13(3) does **not** automatically authorize pistol purchase. Verify exact handgun, license, possession, transfer and local authority permissions. |
| `P2013-S3-Q13` self-defense firearm use | **C** | **Safety-critical original-paper flaw**: 2013 key says BOTH verbal warning and warning shot are universally mandatory before defensive use. Current StGB §32 turns on necessary defense against a present unlawful attack, **NOT** a fixed warning-shot mandate. Firing a warning shot can itself create serious hazards; the archive's C must **not** be treated as tactical instruction or legal guidance. The original printed key is preserved, a **non-spoiling visible warning** appears before answering, and **feedback explicitly says `Matched archival answer only`** rather than unconditional `Correct` if the printed C is selected. |

No hunting-, self-defense-, or firearms-specific permission for the individual reader is implied. German federal statute may interact with state-specific hunting rules, local weapons-office decisions, and U.S. Forces regulations. The legal conclusions above are **scoped to the quoted statutes and practice-paper wording** as actually accessed.
