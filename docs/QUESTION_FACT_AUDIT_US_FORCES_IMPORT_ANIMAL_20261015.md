# GHC 2013 exam audit — U.S. Forces AR-15 import and sick domestic dog (15 October 2026)

## Primary original study-bank source actually checked

The connected Google Drive **`Practice Hunting Test  2013-12-19.pdf`**, [original directly read](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1f_bWpni_W7xpReNHPHCWittNiQSBFOwX/view), `PART III, Weapons and Firearms Law` Q18 and Q19, including its printed **Section 3** answer-key table. Q18: the farmer asks to kill his old sick dog, key **D** (tell the farmer to see a veterinarian). Q19: whether a U.S. Forces hunter can import “their AR15 into Germany,” key **B** (“NO”). These are **historical keyed answers**, not blanket present-day statutory determinations. Readable PDF text checked directly; page pixels were not rendered. Original bank question objects and keyed answers were also retrieved from the live GitHub source.

## Current military and German official evidence checked

1. [Official Army in Europe Regulations index, AE REG 190-6](https://www.aepubs.eur.army.mil/AE-Regulations/smdsearch8659/190-6/), `Registration and Control of Privately Owned Firearms and Other Weapons in Germany`, 5 June 2020, explicitly listed **CURRENT** by Army publications. Official [2020 regulatory PDF](https://media.defense.gov/2020/Jun/05/2002311007/-1/-1/0/AER190-6.PDF) indexed; search-returned introductory text states NATO SOFA does not override German weapons rules and both requirements apply. Direct PDF fetch returned HTTP **403**, so **the rest of the regulation, item-level eligibility and appendices were NOT inspected**, and no claim that a specific AR-15 qualifies is made.
2. [**March 2026 USAREUR-AF Customs Agency official importing weapons pamphlet**](https://home.army.mil/ansbach/1717/7260/6577/Infosheet_for_AE_Form_550-175A_-_March_2026.pdf), **two-page actual PDF opened and page 2 visually inspected**. Page 2 clearly instructs not to bring firearms into Germany until properly registered with U.S. Forces and German authorities. It lists **USAREUR-AF weapons registration, Jagdschein or WBK, and German `Verbringungserlaubnis` (import permit)** to obtain AEA Form 550-175A. It directs U.S. Forces members to the Firearms Section of Motor Vehicle Registration for details.
3. [**2025 21st TSC US Army JAG Legal Informer**](https://www.21tsc.army.mil/Portals/21/JAG/KLI-2025-4%20Fall%20Newsletter.pdf?ver=bcojSWE7IGO2aQwTeE_LdA%3D%3D) publicly indexed as Fall 2025, p.4 `Unlawful Possession of Firearms`: describes the need for both U.S. Forces registration/permission and host nation authority. Original PDF was web-search indexed, but subsequent direct fetch was HTTP **403**; do not treat that as first-hand visual PDF inspection.
4. [German WaffG §29](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/waffg_2002/__29.html), individual import/movement permission and authorized possession prerequisites; [WaffG Anlage 2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/waffg_2002/anlage_2.html) technical classifications, including prohibited/permit-controlled configurations. Official German statute text **directly read**. `AR-15` is a commercial platform designation, not a specification enough to establish if a **particular** model is lawful for a particular owner, magazine/configuration or import. [German Truppenzollgesetz §4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/trzollg/__4.html) separately addresses paperwork/permission for firearms imports under military customs privileges; customs status alone is not German firearms-law possession permission.
5. [German animal welfare `TierSchG §4`](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tierschg/__4.html) imposes killing constraints, pain reduction and competency; [`§17`](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tierschg/__17.html) prohibits unjustified killing of vertebrates. Official federal law current pages directly checked. A sick domestic dog is not game merely because a hunter has a hunting license; veterinary assessment and professional care is the appropriate original paper answer.

## Exact question conclusions

| Original ID | Paper key, preserved | Audit outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `P2013-S3-Q18` domestic sick dog | **D** | **Source-qualified** with German TierSchG §§4 and 17: advise owner to consult a veterinarian, not improvise lethal treatment under hunting privileges. |
| `P2013-S3-Q19` U.S. Forces AR-15 import | **B** | **Historical blanket answer flagged** as *not a current case-by-case determination*: U.S. Forces + German approval, proper advance registration/import permit, and weapon-specific legality required. The original paper `NO` remains; a nonspoiling **pre-answer warning** disclaims the blanket conclusion and archival-only feedback after a correct historical score. Neither **permission to import** nor **a universal current ban** is asserted. Do not confuse a two-round hunting-magazine restriction with German entry/import authorization. |
