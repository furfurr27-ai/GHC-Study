# GHC Study — 2013 firearms-law original paper vs current federal law (15 October 2026)

## Scope, original classroom sources and limits

Five **additional individual** 2013 practice-test records were compared to their own original document and to current published German federal statutes; the bank's original answers, options, stems, IDs, sections, progress identifiers and saved storage are left unchanged. An explanatory correction does **not** imply legal authorization, and the original dated exam questions are not a substitute for local weapons-authority instructions.

**Original test actually inspected**: connected original Google Drive `Practice Hunting Test  2013-12-19.pdf` (file ID `1f_bWpni_W7xpReNHPHCWittNiQSBFOwX`), specifically `PART III Weapons and Firearms Law` questions 1, 3, 4, 8 and 11, plus the original printed `Answer Key` (Section 3). Printed answers: **Q1 C**, **Q3 E**, **Q4 A**, **Q8 C**, **Q11 C**. These are the exact original paper's historical answers, not a new statutory assessment. Source retrieval was readable PDF text; this continuation did not independently inspect its page pixels. A second original copy had been checked previously to resolve the Section 3 Q2 original defect.

**2026 GHC source actually inspected**: `Weapons - Ammo (20260214).pdf` Google Drive ID `1aO-ftzIXRw6acqMYlpb71ZG7nV8hZ1Mp`, especially `Energy` (roe deer 1,000 J at 100 m, other cloven-hoofed 2,000 J at 100 m) and final `SAFETY` guidance. Original GHC slide does not by itself establish present weapons-law storage classes, transport distinctions, or semiautomatic cartridge loading.

**Current official law individually checked**: direct German government legal text at [WaffG §12(3)(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/waffg_2002/__12.html), [AWaffV §13](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/awaffv/__13.html), and [BJagdG §19](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bjagdg/__19.html). Also inspected [SVLFG's official hunting-safety guidance](https://www.svlfg.de/sichere-jagd), including unloading guns in vehicles, hunting district exception around accessibility, and separation of guns and ammunition. These government web sources are independent updates/cross-checks; they do not override the exact wording printed in 2013 when displaying the **historical paper key**.

## Five item-level comparison records

| Question ID | Original paper key | Source comparison and learner change | Source-audit classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| `P2013-S3-Q01` (gun storage) | C | The paper's Waffenschrank principle is correct but incomplete. The `AWaffV §13` container/security grade, certification, unloaded status, amounts, location and exception rules matter today; do not treat a simple lock or a hidden rifle as compliant. | `source_compared_explanation_qualified` |
| `P2013-S3-Q03` (transport: “must ... all above”) | E | The 2013 quiz lists unloaded, locked case, trunk, boxed ammunition in a shared case. `WaffG §12(3)(2)` requires **not ready to fire** and **not readily accessible** for its ordinary transport exception; it does **not** mandate that exact universal trunk-and-case checklist in every circumstance. SVLFG gives specific hunting vehicle and ammunition separation advice. A **pre-answer dated-law warning** now disclaims the question's universal “must” wording. Historical E stays intact. | `source_compared_key_conflict` — dated over-narrow/current law mismatch, *not* a claim that the 2013 paper transcription is wrong |
| `P2013-S3-Q04` (caliber / muzzle energy) | A | Actual question asks about **non-roe cloven-hoofed game**. `BJagdG §19(1)(2)(b)` sets the general minimum **6.5 mm** and **E100 ≥ 2,000 J**; `§19(1)(2)(a)` applies distinct **1,000 J E100** to roe deer. `§19(3)` allows narrowly specified state-recognized exceptions. 2026 GHC ammo lesson matches the 1,000/2,000 J distinction. | `source_compared_explanation_qualified` |
| `P2013-S3-Q08` (rifle car ride home from range) | C | `WaffG §12(3)(2)` supports unloaded **and not readily accessible** rifle during ordinary transport, not simply open bolt or ammo removal alone. Original C stays, with source-qualified explanation. | `source_compared_explanation_qualified` |
| `P2013-S3-Q11` (semiautomatic hunting rifle) | C | 2013 paper restricts magazine to **2 rounds**. Current `BJagdG §19(1)(2)(c)` prohibits hunting with a semiautomatic long gun loaded with **more than 3 cartridges total**, counting the chamber; **nominal magazine capacity alone** is not the statutory rule. A **pre-answer law-change warning** explicitly explains the difference. Historic key C stays, but is **not certified as complete current law**. | `source_compared_key_conflict` — changed/differently framed law |

The qualification is an educational distinction between published sources. Verify any actual firearm acquisition, carrying, transport and usage with applicable federal, Hessian and installation-specific rules and the competent authority.
